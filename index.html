About

I am a master’s student in Network and Information Security at Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications, with expected graduation in June 2027. I am also a Research Assistant at The Chinese University of Hong Kong.

My research spans AI for Security and Security of AI, with work on learning-based vulnerability detection, language model robustness, and the security of AI agents.

I also collaborate on multimodal image retrieval and long-video reasoning. I received my bachelor’s degree in Information Security from Wuhan University of Science and Technology in June 2024.

Research interests

I.

AI for Security

Machine learning and language models for software vulnerability detection, malicious application analysis, and security-related web content analysis.

II.

Security of AI

Security and robustness of language models and AI agents, including adversarial evaluation, deployment environments, cross-origin information use, and hallucination propagation in multi-agent systems.
